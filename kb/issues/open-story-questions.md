# Open story questions

Status: open

The import is a 52-second **series episode 1** ending on a cliffhanger. The
target is a **~5-minute self-contained short**. Questions to answer in
stages 1–3 of `story-bible.md`:

1. **Format**: standalone short with a complete arc, or a pilot that may end
   open? (Determines whether the silver light must be paid off.)
2. **Premise**: what does Elias want, what is his misbelief, and what does the
   film argue (controlling idea)?
3. **The Rose**: who/what is she, why has she waited for Elias, what does she
   want from him? Ally, trickster, or something more ambiguous?
4. **The threat**: what is the silver light? Is "your world is not safe" true,
   a test, or a manipulation?
5. **Antagonism**: who or what opposes Elias? (The import has no opponent.)
6. **Queen Elara**: on- or off-screen? What does she stand for in the theme?
7. **Divergence from the novella**: which turn makes this clearly *not*
   Saint-Exupéry's story?
8. **Ending**: what has changed in Elias by the last image?
