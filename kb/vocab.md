# Vocabulary

Status: provisional

| Canonical | Definition | Aliases | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Elias** / **Prince Elias** | 10-year-old prince of Aurelia, protagonist. | "little prince" (Rose's address to him only) | [import] |
| **the Rose** | The single speaking rose in the forgotten flower bed. Capitalized when it is the character. Not named (yet). | the speaking rose, the talking rose | [import] |
| **Queen Elara** | Elias's mother, ruler of Aurelia. | the Queen, Mother | [import] |
| **Aurelia** | The tiny inhabited planet. | — | [import] |
| **Aurelia Palace** | The royal palace on Aurelia's upper surface. | the palace | [import] |
| **the Royal Garden** | The garden behind Aurelia Palace; main location. | the garden | [import] |
| **the forgotten flower bed** | Circular, overgrown bed at the far edge of the Royal Garden where the Rose grows. | the forgotten corner | [import] |
| **the silver light** | Unexplained light appearing beyond Aurelia's horizon at the end of the import. Meaning undecided. | — | [import] |
| **the signet pin** | Elias's gold pin bearing the royal crest (crest shows a crowned rose in the concept image). | royal crest pin | [import] [image] |

## Avoid

- "The Little Prince" as a name for Elias or as the film title (we only borrow
  the premise).
- "talking flower" (use *the Rose*).
