Status: provisional (appearance decided 2026-10-04) · Sources: [import] §2, [image] `Import/Queen Elara - Character Sheet.png`

## Role
Elias's mother. Brief presence (opening/closing) in the imported episode; not
in any imported shot. Loving and protective but absorbed by royal duty; does
not see how lonely Elias is.

## Appearance — decided (2026-10-04, author: match the character sheet)
- **Face**: fair porcelain skin, warm brown eyes, soft rosy lips, serene,
  gentle expression; looks early-to-mid thirties.
- **Hair**: dark brown-to-black, gathered into a voluminous chignon/updo at
  the back of the head.
- **Jewellery**: small gold tiara set with red-coral stones; coral drop
  earrings; pink bead necklace with a small pendant.
- **Gown**: full-skirted ball gown in **blush-pink** lace and tulle; fitted
  bodice with square neckline and lace panels; three-quarter sleeves ending
  in layered ruffles; skirt with draped tiers and scattered small fabric
  **rosettes** (roses echo the Rose and the crest). No ivory or burgundy.
- **Prop**: slim pink sceptre topped with a heart-shaped gold-and-pink
  ornament.
- Dropped from the import: dark auburn hair, ivory & burgundy gown, "late 30s".
