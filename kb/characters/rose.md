# The Rose

Status: provisional · Sources: [import] §2, [image] `Import/Rose - Character Sheet.png`

## Role
Mysterious guide and catalyst. Knows more about Aurelia than she first reveals.
Has "been waiting a very long time" for Elias. [import]

## Three dimensions
| Dimension | Content |
|---|---|
| Physical | A single rose rooted in the forgotten flower bed; moves without wind; centre glows when she speaks. |
| Social | Unknown origin; relationship to Aurelia and the royal family undefined (crest on Elias's pin is a crowned rose — possible link). |
| Psychological | Wise, mysterious, playful, slightly teasing. Gentle and ancient, never frightening. |

## Voice
Soft, warm, almost whispered, slight magical resonance; "ancient female voice".
Never cartoonish. See [../styles/dialogue-voice.md](../styles/dialogue-voice.md).

## Visual lock (for prompts)
> A single large rose with light-yellow, velvety petals, a softly luminous
> golden centre that glows with a moonlight-like light when she speaks, a gentle
> stylized face within the centre (warm eyes, small mouth), emerald-green
> leaves, a slender dark-green stem with thorns, rooted in garden soil with
> small stones.

Expressions on sheet: gentle/wise, playful/teasing (wink), mysterious/serious,
speaking (glow), wonder, smile.

**Colour — decided (2026-10-04, author):** the Rose is **yellow**
(light-yellow velvety petals, golden centre), as in the concept image. The
