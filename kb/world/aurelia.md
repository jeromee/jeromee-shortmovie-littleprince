# Aurelia (the planet)

Status: provisional · Source: [import] §1, §3

## Facts
- A tiny spherical planet floating in deep space; the curved horizon is visible
  from the palace garden.
- Year: **1830** (European royal aesthetics). Whether "1830" is Earth's
  calendar or Aurelia's own is undecided.
- On its upper surface: a miniature European-style palace (Aurelia Palace),
  gardens, forests, tiny villages.
- Sky: vast blue-black and full of stars even in the brightest daylight. [import]
- Small but believable scale: miniature yet credible.
