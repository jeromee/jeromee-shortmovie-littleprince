# The Royal Garden of Aurelia

Status: provisional · Sources: [import] §3, [image] `Import/Garden with Rose 01.png`

## Description
The garden behind Aurelia Palace — expansive and enchanted despite the
planet's size. Peaceful at first, increasingly mysterious near the Rose.

Elements [import]: ancient stone pathways · rose arches and ivy-covered walls ·
small fountains · tall cypress trees · wildflower beds · an old glass
greenhouse · a low stone wall with the planet's curved horizon beyond · a
star-filled sky.

## Key landmark: the forgotten flower bed
- Circular bed at the far edge of the garden, overgrown, hidden behind an
  ivy-covered wall [import].
- At its centre: the Rose.
