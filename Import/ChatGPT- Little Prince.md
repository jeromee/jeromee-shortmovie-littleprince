# 1) SERIES CONCEPT

**Series Title:** *The Prince and the Rose*  
**Episode 1:** *The Rose That Spoke*

**Genre:** Fantasy adventure, whimsical coming-of-age, mystery  
**Tone:** Magical, intimate, cinematic, gently mysterious  
**Target audience:** Family / children, with enough visual richness to appeal to adults

### Premise
In the year **1830**, on a tiny planet called **Aurelia**, 10-year-old Prince **Elias** lives in a miniature royal kingdom surrounded by an endless starry sky. His world is small enough that he can see its curved horizon from the palace garden.

One quiet morning, while exploring a forgotten corner of the royal garden, Elias discovers an extraordinary crimson rose. Unlike every other flower, the rose can **speak**.

The rose knows things Elias has never been taught—including a secret about his tiny planet and a warning that something is coming.

**Episode 1 story arc:**  
Elias begins as a lonely, curious prince who believes his garden is the safest and most familiar place in the universe. His encounter with the talking rose transforms it into a place of mystery. By the end of the episode, Elias realizes that his discovery is only the beginning of a much larger adventure.

### Visual style
- Storybook fantasy blended with **1830s European royal aesthetics**
- Warm morning sunlight, soft clouds, rich garden colors
- Tiny planet with a visible curved horizon and deep blue space beyond
- Slightly surreal scale: the palace, garden, and planet feel miniature yet believable
- Cinematic depth of field and subtle magical particles
- Elegant, painterly production design with realistic character animation

**Continuity rule for CapCut:** Keep Elias and the rose visually identical in every shot. Elias should always appear approximately 10 years old, and the rose should always have the same deep-crimson petals and distinctive golden center.

# 2) MAIN CHARACTERS

## Prince Elias

### Appearance
10-year-old boy, slender and slightly small for his age, fair skin, soft chestnut-brown hair that falls naturally across his forehead, expressive hazel-green eyes, youthful face, subtle freckles.

### Clothing
1830-inspired royal clothing: deep navy-blue velvet jacket with subtle gold embroidery, cream high-collared shirt, fitted beige trousers, polished brown ankle boots, and a small antique gold signet pin bearing the royal crest.

### Personality
Curious, intelligent, imaginative and kind. Elias is fascinated by things adults dismiss as unimportant. He is brave, but not fearless. He sometimes feels lonely because everyone treats him as a prince before treating him like a child.

### Role
The protagonist. His curiosity leads him into the mystery surrounding the talking rose.

---

## The Rose

### Appearance
A single magnificent rose growing from an otherwise ordinary garden bed. Deep crimson petals with delicate variations in color, a luminous golden center, emerald-green leaves and a slender dark-green stem. It subtly glows when speaking, as if moonlight is trapped inside its petals.

### Clothing
None.

### Personality
Wise, mysterious, playful and slightly teasing. Its voice should feel gentle and ancient rather than frightening. The rose clearly knows more about Aurelia than it initially reveals.

### Role
Elias's mysterious guide and the catalyst for the series' larger adventure.

**Voice direction:** Soft, warm, almost whisper-like, with a slight magical resonance. Never make it sound like a cartoon character.

---

## Queen Elara

### Appearance
Elegant woman in her late 30s, warm complexion, dark auburn hair, intelligent eyes and a calm expression.

### Clothing
1830s royal gown in muted ivory and burgundy, delicate gold jewelry, understated royal styling.

### Personality
Loving and protective, but often distracted by royal responsibilities. She genuinely cares for Elias but doesn't fully understand how lonely he feels.

### Role
Elias's mother. She appears briefly in the opening/closing moments of the episode or can be introduced more fully in later episodes.

---

# 3) MAIN LOCATION

## The Royal Garden of Aurelia

The story takes place primarily in the enormous garden behind **Aurelia Palace**.

Despite the planet being tiny, the garden feels expansive and enchanted. It contains:

- Ancient stone pathways
- Rose arches and ivy-covered walls
- Small fountains
- Tall cypress trees
- Wildflower beds
- An old glass greenhouse
- A forgotten circular flower bed at the far edge of the garden
- A low stone wall beyond which the **curved horizon of the tiny planet** can be seen
- A vast blue-black sky filled with stars, even during the brightest parts of the day

### Key visual landmark
At the center of the forgotten flower bed is the **single crimson talking rose**.

The garden should feel peaceful at first, then gradually become mysterious as Elias approaches the rose.

# 4) SHOT 1 — THE TINY KINGDOM IN THE SKY

### Purpose
Establish the world, the tiny planet, the year 1830 atmosphere, and Elias's unusual home.

### Visual
A sweeping cinematic view of Aurelia: a tiny spherical planet floating in a vast blue-black star field. A miniature European-style royal palace sits on the planet's upper surface, surrounded by gardens, forests and tiny villages.

Transition from the planet into the palace garden where young Prince Elias is walking alone.

### Director Mode Prompt
**“Cinematic fantasy establishing shot of a tiny inhabited planet called Aurelia floating in deep space, 1830 European royal architecture on its surface, elegant miniature palace surrounded by lush gardens and forests, visible curved planetary horizon, warm golden morning sunlight, atmospheric clouds, magical but believable world-building, storybook realism, detailed environment, gentle cinematic movement, transition smoothly from a wide view of the planet down into the royal garden where a 10-year-old prince walks alone, family-friendly fantasy adventure, consistent visual design, realistic lighting, shallow atmospheric haze.”**

### Camera
Begin with an **extreme wide aerial shot** of the planet, slowly push toward the palace, then descend into the garden behind Elias.

### Character present
Prince Elias.

### Duration
**10 seconds**

---

# 5) SHOT 2 — THE FORGOTTEN CORNER

### Purpose
Show Elias's curiosity and lead the viewer toward the discovery.

### Visual
Elias wanders away from the manicured palace gardens and discovers an overgrown section hidden behind an ivy-covered stone wall. He notices one unusual patch of sunlight illuminating a single flower.

He stops.

The garden becomes unusually quiet.

### Director Mode Prompt
**“Young Prince Elias, exactly 10 years old, slender boy with chestnut-brown hair, hazel-green eyes and subtle freckles, wearing a deep navy 1830s velvet royal jacket with gold embroidery, cream high-collared shirt, beige trousers and brown ankle boots, exploring a forgotten corner of the royal garden. He pushes aside hanging ivy and discovers an overgrown circular flower bed. Morning sunlight forms a mysterious beam across one single crimson rose. The surrounding garden is still and quiet. Elias looks curious and slightly surprised, moving cautiously toward the flower. Cinematic fantasy realism, detailed 1830 garden design, soft golden light, magical atmosphere, natural child performance, subtle suspense.”**

### Camera
**Medium tracking shot** following Elias from behind, then slowly move around to a **close three-quarter shot** of his face as he notices the rose.

### Character present
Prince Elias.

### Duration
**8 seconds**

---

# 6) SHOT 3 — THE ROSE

### Purpose
Introduce the magical centerpiece and create the episode's first major moment of wonder.

### Visual
Elias kneels beside the rose.

He reaches toward it but stops before touching it.

The rose gently moves despite there being no wind.

Its petals begin to glow faintly.

Elias whispers:

**“Are you... alive?”**

The rose slowly turns toward him.

### Director Mode Prompt
**“Intimate cinematic close-up in a magical 1830 royal garden. Prince Elias, a 10-year-old boy with chestnut-brown hair, hazel-green eyes, freckles and a navy velvet royal jacket, kneels beside a single extraordinary crimson rose. The rose has elegant deep-red petals, emerald leaves, a slender dark-green stem and a subtle luminous golden center. No wind is blowing, yet the rose slowly turns toward Elias. Its petals emit a very soft warm golden glow. Elias looks astonished but fascinated, not frightened. He quietly asks, ‘Are you alive?’ The rose pauses, then gently moves as if preparing to answer. Emotional cinematic lighting, shallow depth of field, realistic facial animation, magical realism, highly detailed petals, gentle suspense, family fantasy.”**

### Camera
Start with a **macro close-up of the rose**, slowly rack focus from the petals to Elias's eyes. Finish with a very slow push-in toward the rose's face-like orientation.

### Character present
Prince Elias and the Rose.

### Duration
**10 seconds**

---

# 7) SHOT 4 — THE ROSE SPEAKS

### Purpose
Deliver the episode's central reveal and establish the mystery of the series.

### Visual
The rose speaks for the first time.

Its petals gently open and close as it talks, while its golden center pulses subtly with light.

**Rose:**  
“Of course I am alive, little prince.”

Elias freezes.

He looks around the garden to see if someone is playing a trick on him.

**Elias:**  
“You... you can talk?”

The rose responds:

**“I have been waiting a very long time for you.”**

### Director Mode Prompt
**“Cinematic magical dialogue scene between a 10-year-old prince and a beautiful talking crimson rose in an enchanted 1830 royal garden. Keep Prince Elias's appearance exactly consistent: chestnut-brown hair, hazel-green eyes, freckles, navy-blue velvet jacket with gold embroidery, cream shirt, beige trousers. The rose remains the same deep crimson rose with emerald leaves and a softly glowing golden center. The rose gently moves its petals as it speaks with a warm ancient female voice: ‘Of course I am alive, little prince.’ Elias freezes in astonishment, looks around to check whether someone is hiding nearby, then looks back at the flower and says, ‘You... you can talk?’ The rose softly glows and answers: ‘I have been waiting a very long time for you.’ Emotional performances, subtle magical particles, warm morning sunlight, shallow depth of field, cinematic fantasy realism, elegant pacing, mysterious but comforting atmosphere.”**

### Camera
Alternate between **close-up on Elias** and **extreme close-up on the rose**, ending on a slow two-shot showing them together.

### Character present
Prince Elias and the Rose.

### Duration
**12 seconds**

---

# 8) SHOT 5 — THE WARNING

### Purpose
End Episode 1 with a strong cliffhanger that makes the audience want Episode 2.

### Visual
Elias leans closer.

The playful warmth of the rose disappears.

Its golden glow becomes dimmer.

Beyond the garden wall, the sky suddenly darkens in one distant area.

The rose whispers:

**“You must listen carefully, Elias. Your little world is not as safe as you believe.”**

Elias looks toward the horizon.

A strange **silver light** appears beyond the curved edge of Aurelia.

Cut to black.

### Director Mode Prompt
**“Cinematic fantasy cliffhanger. Prince Elias, exactly 10 years old and visually identical to previous shots, kneels beside the talking crimson rose in the royal garden of tiny planet Aurelia. The warm morning atmosphere slowly changes as the rose becomes serious and its golden center dims. The rose quietly tells Elias, ‘You must listen carefully, Elias. Your little world is not as safe as you believe.’ Elias slowly turns toward the distant curved horizon of the tiny planet. Beyond the garden wall, the sky darkens in one small distant region and an unexplained silver light appears beyond the horizon. Elias looks concerned and amazed. The rose remains motionless. Slow dramatic camera movement, subtle wind through leaves, cinematic shadows, mysterious silver light, elegant magical realism, family-friendly fantasy, no horror, ending on a powerful visual cliffhanger. Fade to black.”**

### Camera
Begin with a **close two-shot** of Elias and the rose. Slowly pull backward as Elias turns toward the horizon. Continue pulling back until the characters appear small against the enormous sky. Hold on the mysterious silver light, then **fade to black**.

### Character present
Prince Elias and the Rose.

### Duration
**12 seconds**
